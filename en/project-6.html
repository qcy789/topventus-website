Hotel "Palace Dushanbe"

Dushanbe · 2024 · 18,000 m², 220 rooms

About the project

A 5-star hotel in the center of Dushanbe with 220 rooms, featuring restaurants, conference halls, a spa center, and an indoor pool.

The task.

High comfort requirements (noise in rooms <30 dBA), individual control in each room, aesthetic appearance of diffusers (matching interior design).

Solution

Diffusers with individual shower valves, painted in interior color RAL 9010. Silencers on all main sections. Dampers with electric actuators for the central control system.

Result

Noise measurements in the rooms — 26-28 dBA (below the 5-star standard). The hotel opened on schedule and received its 5-star certificate.
