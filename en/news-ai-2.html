Smoke removal systems at Tashkent metro station: technical solutions and safety standards

We examine the engineering aspects of smoke-control ventilation in the Tashkent Metro: requirements, equipment, dampers, and installation specifics. We review the role of EAC certification and TopVentus' experience in supplying solutions for Central Asia.

The metro is not only the fastest mode of urban transport but also a facility with special safety requirements. Underground stations and tunnels are enclosed spaces where evacuation during a fire critically depends on the operation of engineering systems. Given the dense urban development of Tashkent and the growing passenger flow, the modernization of smoke exhaust systems is becoming a priority task for the city's infrastructure.

Modern design standards (including GOST and SNiP RUz) impose strict requirements on smoke-control ventilation. The system must not only remove combustion products but also create air pressurization in stairwells and vestibules, ensuring smoke-free evacuation routes. For engineers and contractors working on projects in Uzbekistan and neighboring republics, it is critically important to understand the specifics of equipment selection and its certification.

Specifics of smoke-control ventilation in the metro

Unlike ordinary industrial buildings, the ventilation of metro tunnels operates in an aggressive environment: high humidity, pressure fluctuations from train movement, vibration, and dustiness. The smoke removal systems at Tashkent metro station must provide:

  • Smoke removal from tunnels and platform sectionswith an air flow rate sufficient to maintain visibility on evacuation routes.
  • Air supplyin elevator shafts, escalator tunnels, and stairwells to prevent the spread of smoke.
  • High temperature resistance(up to 400°C for 60 minutes or more) is a key requirement for fire-resistant elements.

The key role here is assigned to fire dampers and axial fans. The dampers must have a standardized fire resistance limit (EI 60, EI 90) and low aerodynamic resistance. At the same time, the equipment must be adapted to local operating conditions and the climate of Central Asia.

Technical solutions: from valves to fans

Designing smoke removal systems for the Tashkent Metro requires a comprehensive approach. The main components to consider when upgrading or constructing new stations are:

  1. Fire Dampers for smoke exhaust.Installed in air ducts crossing fire barriers. For metro applications, valves with electric actuators are used, providing remote control from the dispatch center. Tightness class "dense" is important to prevent air leaks in normal operation mode.
  2. Smoke exhaust fans.Axial or centrifugal units capable of operating at high temperatures. For tunnels, reversible axial fans are often used, allowing for directed air movement to evacuate people to a safe zone.
  3. Automation systems.Smoke detectors, controllers, and actuators must be integrated into the station's overall fire alarm system.

Special attention should be paid to air ducts. For metro tunnels, welded air ducts made of galvanized or black steel with increased density are used, since air leaks are unacceptable here — this directly affects the efficiency of smoke extraction.

Certification and compliance with EAC standards

For the supply of ventilation equipment to facilities in Uzbekistan, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, and Tajikistan, a mandatory requirement is the availability of the EAEU (EAC) certificate of conformity. This applies not only to fans but also to fire dampers, which are subject to assessment under the technical regulation "On Fire Safety Requirements" (EAEU TR 043/2017).

TopVentus, with 16 years of experience in manufacturing ventilation equipment, supplies solutions certified to standardsCE, EAC, CCCF, and ISO 9001. The presence of these certificates guarantees the customer in Tashkent that the equipment has passed fire resistance tests and complies with international safety standards. For contractors, this means smooth passage of state expert review and commissioning of the facility without delays.

It is important to understand: the use of equipment without an EAC certificate at metro facilities is not permitted. This can lead not only to administrative fines, but also to the suspension of construction work. Therefore, when choosing a supplier, it is necessary to verify the availability of valid declarations and certificates for specific equipment models.

Practical recommendations for engineers and contractors

Based on the experience of implementing projects in Central Asia, several key recommendations can be identified for the design and installation of smoke removal systems at metro stations:

  • Calculation based on CFD modeling.For tunnels with complex geometry, it is recommended to perform computer modeling of smoke propagation to accurately determine the required fan performance and valve installation locations.
  • Selecting equipment with a temperature margin.Do not skimp by choosing valves with a fire resistance rating of EI 60 if the standards require EI 90. It is better to build in a safety margin from the very start.
  • Vibration check.Metro equipment must be designed for increased vibration loads. Use vibration isolators and flexible inserts during installation.
  • Pay attention to the climatic modification.For Uzbekistan and Kazakhstan, high summer temperatures and dusty air are relevant, which requires the installation of protective grilles and filters on intake devices.

It is also worth considering that the installation of smoke removal systems in an operating metro is often carried out during nighttime "windows," when train traffic is stopped. This imposes restrictions on logistics and requires the equipment supplier to meet clear delivery deadlines.

Conclusion

Smoke removal systems at the Tashkent metro station are a complex engineering complex requiring highly reliable equipment and precise calculations. Key success factors include compliance with EAC standards, the use of certified fire dampers and specialized fans, as well as professional installation.
