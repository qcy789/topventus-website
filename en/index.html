Ventilation equipment from manufacturer TopVentus

TopVentus is a Chinese manufacturer of ventilation equipment since 2008. We produceadjustable dampers, ceiling diffusers, Duct silencers, fire dampers 70°C, smoke extraction valves 280°Candair duct components.

Our products are exported toKazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistanand other countries of Central Asia, Europe, and Africa. All products have certificatesCE, EAC, CCCF, which ensures hassle-free customs clearance in EAEU countries.
